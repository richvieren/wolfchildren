THE BOOK

It started with three little sisters.

Wolf Children started as a self published coffee table book in Belgium & The Netherlands. Shot on the backdrop of a beautiful organic farm, documenting the barefoot life of three little sisters. It became a hit and sold thousands of copies. It’s always been a dream to expand the wolfpack world wide, and I believe the world needs it now more than ever. New chapters coming soon.
