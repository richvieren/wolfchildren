What it needs from you

The birth time, three places and three answers.

  1. The birth time

    Required for this reading

    Places change the rising sign and the angles, which come from the birth time, so this reading needs it. The birth certificate or the hospital record usually has it.

  2. Three places

    Chosen from a list. Places at a similar longitude to the birthplace read almost the same; a place well to the east or west shows the most change.

  3. Why these three

    Why these three places? Has your child been to any of them, and what were they like there?
