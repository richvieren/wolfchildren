02How They Take Things In

See how their mind processes information and environments.

In the reading: Three lines

Three lines
DreamerDoer
Ideas arrive in Nora long before any hand moves toward them, and they arrive fully formed enough to be interesting on their own. She can spend a long stretch inside something imagined without needing it to become real, so the gap between wanting a thing and starting it stays wide, and the wanting itself is the part that holds her attention.
StarterFinisher
Some things Nora carries all the way to the end and others she leaves standing once the interesting part is done. The deciding factor sits in how much the thing holds her, so her follow-through looks inconsistent until you see what she is choosing between.
SettledAdaptable
Nora holds on to certain fixed points and lets a good deal else move around them without much trouble. She takes most changes in stride, though a few particular things matter more than their size suggests, and those are where she digs in.

06The Closing Question

A reflective question written for you, the parent.

In the reading: A question to sit with

A question to sit with

Undercurrents get noticed by Nora long before anyone names them, and the noticing rarely becomes a comment. Mercury in the eighth house draws the mind toward what is hidden, unsaid or only half explained. The question worth holding is how much she has already worked out that she has decided to keep to herself.
