Before the details

Nothing has been made yet. A full refund.

A reading is written for one child from the details you give us. Before you submit those details, nothing has been made. Write to hello@wolfchildren.co and we refund the purchase in full.

After the details

The reading is being written. The purchase is final.

Once you submit the details, the reading is being written for your child and the purchase is final.

If you are in the EU or UK, you agree to this at checkout, and you give up the 14-day right of withdrawal for digital content by asking us to start.

A mistake of ours

We redo it or refund it. Your choice.

If the reading arrives with a mistake that is ours, such as a wrong chart from a correct birth entry, a missing section, or a file that does not open, write to hello@wolfchildren.co within 30 days of delivery and we redo it or refund it, your choice.

A wrong birth entry

One rewrite at no cost.

If you entered the birth details wrong, write to us within 30 days of submitting them and we rewrite the reading once at no cost, from the corrected details.

Late delivery

Not delivered within 48 hours? Refund on request.

We email you when the reading is ready, within 24 hours of your details arriving. If 48 hours pass and nothing has been delivered, write to us and we refund the purchase on request.

How refunds arrive

Back to the card you paid with.

Refunds go back to the card you paid with, through Stripe, within 5 to 10 working days. We confirm by email when it is sent.
